Security Best Practices

01

Length Matters

Use at least 12-16 characters. Longer passwords exponentially increase the time needed to crack them.

02

Mix Character Types

Combine uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters to maximize entropy and complexity.

03

Avoid Common Patterns

Don't use dictionary words, personal info, keyboard patterns (qwerty), or sequential numbers (123456).

04

Unique Per Account

Never reuse passwords across accounts. A breach on one site shouldn't compromise all your accounts.

05

Use Passphrases

Combine random words into a passphrase like "correct-horse-battery-staple" for both strength and memorability.

06

Enable 2FA/MFA

Always enable two-factor authentication. Even strong passwords can be compromised through phishing or data breaches.
