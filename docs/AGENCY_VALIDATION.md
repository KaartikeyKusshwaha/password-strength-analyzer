# Agency validation before connector expansion

Status: zero completed interviews, zero pain reports, zero design partners.
This document is an interview script, not market-validation evidence.

## Ask about the last real event

1. Describe the last employee/freelancer departure or client handoff. When was it?
2. List each client and system the person could access. Where did that list come
   from? What was uncertain or discovered afterward?
3. Who removed access, how long did it take, and what did they actually do?
4. Which accounts were shared passwords versus named/delegated/partner access?
5. What could you confirm afterward? Were sessions, API keys or recovery factors
   missed? Ask for an anonymized checklist, not credentials or private exports.
6. Did a client request access evidence/security questionnaires? What exactly
   did they request, and did the existing process satisfy it?
7. What tools and staff time are paid for today? What alternative was tried?
8. Which single system would need integration before this product is useful?
9. Would you run a test-data offboarding with us? Who approves participation?
10. After testing, what would justify trying a real event and paying a flat team
    price? Record their answer; do not lead with an assumed price or feature list.

Avoid "Would you use an AI security product?" as demand evidence. Separate a
specific costly/uncertain event from polite interest in a demo.

## Per-interview record

- Date, agency pseudonym, role, permission to retain notes.
- Team size and number of clients (optional ranges).
- Recent event and affected system list.
- Current process, time/cost, actual failure/uncertainty.
- Short attributed notes or consented quotation.
- Shared-password/delegated access split.
- Proof-pack demand: actual client request / hypothetical / none.
- Connector ranked first and why; required admin/API access availability.
- Pain conclusion with reasons: real / unclear / absent.
- Test-data design-partner agreement and next step, if explicitly offered.

Keep private interview notes outside Git, with retention agreed by the owner.
Publish only consented anonymized aggregates. Do not store customer secrets.

## Gates

- Interview 5–10 owners; at least three must describe real offboarding/client
  access pain, backed by concrete events.
- Recruit 2–3 explicitly willing design partners. A contact list is not a partner.
- Rank connectors by their actual departing-person system inventories. Google
  Workspace is the first planned connector; the next platform follows evidence.
- Do not proceed to connector expansion if fewer than three pain reports exist.
- Later require one real client handoff and weekly review by a partner, after
  test-data validation and the security-review gate for valuable credentials.
- Pricing and proof-pack demand remain hypotheses until these records support them.
